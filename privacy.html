Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information SecureShare collects when you use the service, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the website and to shares created through it.

Information we collect

  • Content you share. The files or text you upload, held only until the share expires or is retrieved, whichever you configured.
  • Share metadata. The lifetime, access settings and a record of retrieval, used to operate and secure the share.
  • Technical data. Basic request information such as IP address and timestamps, used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

How we use it

We use this information solely to deliver and protect the sharing service: to generate and verify codes, enforce expiry, prevent abuse, and keep the service reliable. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Shared content and its metadata are deleted automatically when the share expires or reaches its access limit. Technical logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes, then removed.

Your choices

You control the lifetime and access settings of every share. To ask about data associated with a share, use the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page.
