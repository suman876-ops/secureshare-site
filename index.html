Features

Privacy-first sharing, end to end

Every share is protected by verification, a short lifetime, and tight limits on who can retrieve it.

Secure OTP Verification

Recipients unlock content with a one-time code, so a leaked link alone is not enough.

Automatic Expiration

Set a lifetime from minutes to days; content is removed automatically once it lapses.

One-Time Access

Burn the link after a single successful retrieval so content is seen exactly once.

Password Protection

Layer a password on top of the OTP when you need an extra gate on sensitive content.

How it works

Four steps from upload to retrieval

A straightforward flow that keeps the sender in control and the recipient verified.

01

Upload or type

Add a file or paste the text you want to share securely.

02

Generate a link

Get a protected share link plus a one-time password.

03

Share it

Send the link and the OTP to your recipient over separate channels.

04

Retrieve safely

The recipient unlocks the content before it expires.
