Security

SecureShare is designed around a simple principle: the link on its own should never be enough to reach your content.

Security architecture

Built so that the link alone is never enough

SecureShare pairs every share with a verification step, a short lifetime, and tight limits on who can retrieve it.

  • One-time passwords generated with a cryptographically secure source and short expiry.
  • Rate limiting on OTP attempts to slow down guessing.
  • File type and size validation before anything is stored.
  • Unguessable share links, with keys and secrets kept on the server.
  • Automatic deletion of expired files and their metadata records.

How protection works

Every share is bound to a one-time password that expires quickly and can only be used a limited number of times. Attempts are rate-limited so a code cannot be guessed by brute force, and the share itself disappears once its lifetime ends.

Uploaded content is validated for type and size before it is accepted, and share links are generated from a cryptographically random source so they cannot be predicted or enumerated.

Data handling

Expired shares are removed from storage along with their database records. Nothing is retained beyond the lifetime you set. For details on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue, please contact us via the contact page before disclosing it publicly.
